# RM6241 Housing Maintenance and Repair DPS

**Assessment date:** 27 July 2026

**Agreement:** RM6241 Housing Maintenance and Repair Dynamic Purchasing System

**Current route:** Supplier Registration Service

**Commercial decision:** `PREPARE - APPLY ONLY FOR EVIDENCED SERVICES`

## Executive Decision

RM6241 is open to new suppliers and currently runs to 23 February 2029. Admission is free, but appointment does not guarantee any work. Buyers use the DPS filters to shortlist suppliers and then run further competitions.

This is primarily a housing maintenance and repair DPS. It is not a general commercial-cleaning framework. UCM should apply only if it can evidence relevant void, property-care, minor works or housing-portfolio services. It must not select electrical, gas, asbestos, fire, water-hygiene, retrofit or survey services without the required competent staff, systems and certifications.

CHAS or another SSIP certificate is not a stated RM6241 admission requirement. A buyer may require SSIP in a later competition, but UCM should not delay the admission work solely to obtain CHAS.

## Insurance Discrepancy

One DPSQ screen lists Employers' Liability at GBP 5 million and Public Liability and Professional Indemnity at GBP 1 million. The DPS Needs document and governing insurance schedule require GBP 5 million for all three.

UCM must plan against the higher contractual requirement:

- Employers' Liability: GBP 5 million.
- Public Liability: GBP 5 million.
- Professional Indemnity: GBP 5 million.

Before submitting, request written confirmation from the broker that UCM's insured activities cover the exact services selected. If the GBP 5 million Professional Indemnity requirement is commercially disproportionate, send a clarification through the Supplier Registration Service. Do not assume the lower questionnaire screen overrides the contract.

## Financial Viability

UCM must upload the completed Financial Viability Risk Assessment tool and three years of published accounts. If any ratio is Amber or Red, this is not an automatic failure, but a specific explanation and credible mitigation are mandatory.

Important thresholds in the supplied tool include:

- Turnover above two times the approximate annual contract value for a Green result.
- Positive net assets for a Green result.
- Acid ratio above 1.0 for a Green result.
- Operating margin above 8% for a Green result.
- Net debt to EBITDA below 2.5 times for a Green result.

The tool uses an approximate annual contract value of GBP 10,000. The accountant should complete or verify all figures. If the filed accounts do not contain enough profit-and-loss or cash-flow detail, obtain management accounts or the relevant supporting figures from the accountant.

No accounts, bank information, tax records or completed FVRA workbook may be stored in this public repository.

## Service Selection

### Select only when UCM can evidence delivery

- Void/Vacant Management, limited to the exact services UCM genuinely performs.
- General Refurbishments, if supported by similar-work evidence and competent delivery capacity.
- Painting and Decorating, if this is an established insured service.
- General Building Works, only where UCM can define boundaries, competent supervision and subcontractor controls.
- Damp or mould remedial action only after method, product claims, competence, insurance and disposal controls are verified.

### Do not select now

- Whole Home Retrofit Services.
- PAS 2030 or TrustMark-dependent services.
- Asbestos removal or surveys.
- Gas, electrical, fire, lift or water-hygiene compliance services.
- Technical surveys or certifications UCM is not qualified to issue.
- Housing Portfolio Management unless UCM can provide the required centralised repair-request, appointment, move-in/out, disposal and quality-management service.
- All locations or national coverage.
- Contract values of GBP 5 million or more.

### Initial location filters

Start with the London subregions UCM can mobilise and supervise reliably. Add neighbouring counties only after Carlos confirms real travel, staffing and response capacity.

### Initial value filters

Recommended starting bands:

- Below GBP 10,000.
- GBP 10,001 to GBP 19,999.
- GBP 20,000 to GBP 49,999.
- GBP 50,000 to GBP 99,999 only after capacity and working-capital review.

Filters can be updated later. Broad selections do not create capability and can expose UCM during a buyer competition.

## What Is Not Required Today

The pack contains JCT and NEC contract forms, TUPE schedules, implementation plans, service levels, business continuity, exit management, background checks, security schedules, pricing templates and order forms. These are mainly call-off documents.

Do not complete them for admission unless the online RM6241 application explicitly requests them. Build reusable versions later, before bidding for a buyer's live requirement.

## Buyer Competition Readiness

Appointment only creates access. Before responding to a buyer, UCM still needs:

- A priced and margin-checked service model.
- Mobilisation and staffing plan.
- Relevant case studies and references.
- RAMS and COSHH evidence.
- Quality-control and Proof Passport process.
- Complaint, escalation and continuity arrangements.
- Resident safeguarding and role-appropriate background checks where required.
- TUPE support when an incumbent workforce may transfer.
- Social-value commitments linked to measurable delivery.
- Carbon actions based on evidence rather than unsupported environmental claims.
