## Purpose

This is the single Level 1 procurement control file for UCM.

It records:

- the basic information Carlos has been asked to provide;
- what has already been found from public UCM sources;
- what the delivery team can create after Carlos supplies the facts;
- registration actions for the first supplier portals;
- the next action, owner and evidence location for every item.

This register is intentionally smaller than the full supplier-evidence guide. It is designed to make UCM credible for proportionate small-contract and subcontract opportunities without pretending that major-framework readiness is required now.

## Security Rule

This GitHub repository is public. Do not upload:

- bank details;
- tax references;
- accounts;
- insurance certificates containing sensitive information;
- employee records;
- DBS information;
- client contracts;
- personal contact details that are not already public.

The actual evidence belongs in a private UCM-controlled document library. This file records the status and private filename only.

## RM6241 Housing Maintenance And Repair DPS

**Decision:** `PREPARE - APPLY ONLY FOR EVIDENCED SERVICES`

The supplied RM6241 bid pack has been reviewed. This is primarily a housing maintenance and repair route, not a general commercial-cleaning framework. UCM should use it only for services it can prove, such as relevant void, refurbishment, decorating or minor-works delivery.

Priority admission gates:

- valid D-U-N-S details matching the Supplier Registration Service record;
- Equality and Diversity Policy and Supplier Code acceptance;
- Employers' Liability, Public Liability and Professional Indemnity at GBP 5 million;
- Cyber Essentials Basic;
- completed Financial Viability Risk Assessment tool and three years of accounts;
- social-value commitment and reporting;
- carefully limited London, building-type, service and contract-value filters.

CHAS or another SSIP certificate is not stated as an RM6241 admission requirement. It may still be requested by an individual buyer at call-off.

UCM should initially select contract-value bands only up to GBP 99,999, subject to capacity and working-capital review. It must not select GBP 5 million-plus bands, unsupported national coverage, Whole Home Retrofit or regulated technical services.

See [RM6241 application assessment](rm6241-application-assessment-2026-07-27.md) for the full decision, blockers and submission sequence.

Creating a Coupa account does not make UCM an approved supplier to every organisation using Coupa. Buyer invitations, qualification questions and sourcing events remain buyer-specific.

## Decision Rules

- Do not submit a tender until insurance, price and delivery capacity have been checked.
- Do not use a client name, logo, reference or photograph without permission.
- Do not claim a certificate, registration or policy is complete until the evidence is stored privately and reviewed.
- Do not pursue a contract that requires unsupported school, clinical, technical, disinfection or national-delivery capability.
- Do not price from a competitor's rate. Price from UCM's real labour, supervision, product, equipment, travel, risk and margin costs.
- Do not upload sensitive procurement evidence to this public repository.
