# UCM ProContract Category Selection

**Status:** Registration in progress

**Source reviewed:** ProContract Areas of Interest screen supplied by UCM on 24 July 2026

**Decision:** Do not accept the automatic list generated from the single keyword `cleaning`. It includes unrelated machinery, medical products, agriculture, sewers, pipelines, dry-cleaning equipment and other categories UCM does not currently sell.

## Six Search Keywords

Use these as the starting search terms:

1. `office cleaning`
2. `commercial cleaning`
3. `building cleaning`
4. `communal area cleaning`
5. `deep cleaning`
6. `window cleaning` - only if Carlos confirms current delivery competence and insurance

If window cleaning is not currently insured and deliverable, replace it with `end of tenancy cleaning`.

## Categories To Remove

Remove automatic matches for:

- agricultural, seed, grain, rice and produce-cleaning machinery;
- dental, optical, wound, enteral, endoscopic and other medical products;
- sterilisation, pre-sterilisation and clinical decontamination equipment;
- laboratory equipment and cleaning brushes;
- sewer, gully, pipeline, well, tank and reservoir cleaning;
- street, beach, vehicle and transport-equipment cleaning;
- dry-cleaning machines, laundry equipment and textile or fur cleaning;
- cleaning products, machines, carts and consumables where UCM is not selling goods;
- chimney, air-duct, furnace and high-pressure specialist work;
- integrated catering, security and offshore-platform management;
- any category selected only because it includes the word `cleaning`.

## Region Of Supply

Do not leave `United Kingdom` selected unless Carlos confirms national mobilisation and pricing capacity.

Use:

- `London` - select now.
- `South East England` - select only if Carlos confirms the practical service radius, travel cost and supervision capacity.

This reduces irrelevant alerts and prevents UCM appearing to claim national coverage.

## Final Registration Steps

1. Replace the automatic selections with the core list above.
2. Add conditional categories only after Carlos confirms them.
3. Change the supply region from United Kingdom to London.
4. Review every category once more before clicking `Continue registration`.
5. Save the registration confirmation privately.
6. Configure email alerts using the selected cleaning-service categories.
7. Record the completed registration date in the UCM Tender Tracker.
