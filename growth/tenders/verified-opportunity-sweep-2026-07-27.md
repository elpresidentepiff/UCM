## Commercial Verdict

Seven credible routes were found. They are not equal.

The best immediate Level 1 bid is **Haringey Schools Lot 3, Crowland**. The most useful immediate influence exercise is **Peabody's estate and window-cleaning market engagement**. The most relevant open admission route is **Home Group Part 3 Voids**.

## Opportunity Detail

### Peabody: Estate And Window Cleaning

- **Scope:** Communal areas, windows, bulk waste, bins, graffiti, possible void cleaning and periodic/deep cleans across South London and North East London.
- **Estimated value:** GBP 22 million excluding VAT; GBP 26.4 million including VAT.
- **Status:** Preliminary market engagement, not yet a tender.
- **Route:** Free [ProcureCo supplier registration](https://procureco.uk/supplier-signup/), then request access to the [Peabody project group](https://procureco.uk/groups/peabody-estates-and-window-cleaning-services-contract/).
- **Official notice:** [066912-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/066912-2026).
- **UCM angle:** Ask for geographic lots accessible to SMEs, proportionate turnover tests, proof-led quality reporting and sensible technology requirements.

### Haringey Schools: Lot 3 Crowland

- **Scope:** Cleaning services for Crowland Primary School.
- **Estimated lot value:** GBP 300,000 excluding VAT; GBP 360,000 including VAT over up to five years.
- **Stage 1:** Selection Questionnaire, scored 80% operational and 20% financial. The five highest-scoring suppliers progress.
- **Route:** [mytenders](https://www.mytenders.co.uk/).
- **Official notice:** [063237-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/063237-2026).
- **UCM angle:** This is the closest match to the current Level 1 strategy. Do not submit until the SQ confirms insurance, safeguarding, references, mobilisation, workforce and any TUPE requirements.

### Home Group: Part 3 Voids

- **Scope:** Void repairs, cleans, gas and electrical safety work and EPC-related services. UCM should select only evidenced scope.
- **Admission:** Open until 30 April 2029; SME suitable; maximum supplier fee stated as 0%.
- **Application:** Download the pack from the [official notice](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/039092-2026) and email the completed application to `Dynamic.Markets@homegroup.org.uk`.
- **Future competitions:** [Housing Procurement Portal](https://www.housingprocurement.com/).
- **UCM angle:** Strong route for Handover Complete if cleaning and minor-repair boundaries are documented honestly.

### Guildford: Window And General Cleaning

- **Scope:** General cleaning of eight sports pavilions weekly; separate window-cleaning requirement across about 45 sites.
- **Award weighting:** Quality 55%, social value 5%, price 40%.
- **Route:** [SE Shared Services / In-Tend](https://www.in-tendhost.co.uk/sesharedservices/aspx/Home).
- **Official notice:** [063877-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/063877-2026).
- **UCM angle:** Assess general cleaning first. The notice describes three bidding options but publishes two lot sections, so obtain written clarification before submission.

### Luton: Specialist Deep Cleaning AT1782

- **Scope:** Communal, end-of-tenancy, post-eviction, biohazard, contaminated-waste, emergency and temporary-accommodation cleans.
- **Estimated value:** GBP 240,000 excluding VAT; GBP 288,000 including VAT.
- **Award weighting:** Quality 70%, price 30%; SME suitable.
- **Route:** [ProContract / Due North](https://www.procontract.due-north.com/).
- **Official notice:** [061256-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/061256-2026).
- **UCM angle:** Good geography and value, but hazardous-waste competence is non-negotiable. HOCl promotion does not prove biohazard, sharps or waste compliance.

### Moat: Communal Cleaning DN817122

- **London and Sussex lot:** GBP 3.821 million excluding VAT; GBP 4.586 million including VAT over up to six years.
- **Scope:** Communal cleaning, waste reporting, legal disposal, RAMS and gutter cleaning.
- **Route:** [Housing Procurement Portal](https://www.housingprocurement.com/), reference `DN817122`.
- **Official notice:** [068495-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/068495-2026).
- **UCM angle:** Strong service fit but roughly GBP 637,000 excluding VAT per year at maximum duration. Confirm the financial threshold, TUPE data, mobilisation requirement and safe contract value before deciding whether to bid or partner.

### SE Trains: Fleet Cleaning Market Engagement

- **Future scale:** Approximately 378 units at 44 locations; estimated GBP 100 million to GBP 130 million over six years.
- **Scope:** Heavy, overnight, turnaround, onboard and exterior cleans.
- **Workshop:** Ashford Train Depot, 10 September 2026; attendance confirmation requested by 28 August.
- **Contact:** `molly.weston@southeasternrailway.co.uk`.
- **Official notice:** [070294-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/070294-2026).
- **UCM angle:** Intelligence, pilot or subcontract positioning only. A prime bid at this scale would not be credible.
