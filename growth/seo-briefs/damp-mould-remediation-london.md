## H2: Damp and mould needs more than a cosmetic clean

Visible mould is often the symptom of a wider moisture, ventilation, condensation, leak or building-fabric issue. Simply wiping the surface may improve appearance temporarily without addressing why the problem developed.

UCM takes a practical property-services approach. We assess the reported condition, complete the agreed cleaning and remediation works, identify obvious contributing issues within our scope, document the work and escalate further repairs where required.

This helps landlords and property managers move from a recurring complaint to a clearer record of what was found, what was completed and what action may still be needed.

## H2: Supporting landlord response under Awaab's Law

Awaab's Law introduced statutory response requirements for specified hazards in social housing, including significant damp and mould hazards.

UCM's damp and mould remediation, responsive maintenance and evidence-capture services can form part of a landlord's operational response. We can attend instructed works, record condition, complete agreed remediation and maintenance activities, provide photographic evidence and update the client on completion or further action required.

UCM should not be described as "Awaab's Law certified" unless a recognised certification exists and UCM genuinely holds it. Response-time commitments published on this page should only be added where UCM has formally agreed and can operationally guarantee them.

## H2: Occupied homes and resident-sensitive working

Damp and mould works often take place in occupied homes. Operatives may be entering bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens and other personal living spaces.
