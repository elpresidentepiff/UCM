## H2: Damp and mould in social housing

Damp and mould requires careful operational handling because it affects resident health, property condition, complaints and statutory responsibilities.

UCM can provide instructed damp and mould treatment, specialist cleaning, associated maintenance within scope, occupied-home working and photographic evidence.

Where the visible problem indicates a leak, ventilation issue, building-fabric defect or other cause requiring further work, the issue can be recorded and escalated rather than treated as a purely cosmetic clean.

## H2: Supporting landlord response under Awaab's Law

Awaab's Law introduced statutory response requirements for specified hazards in social housing, including significant damp and mould hazards.

UCM's role is operational: attend instructed works, complete agreed cleaning/remediation/maintenance tasks, record condition, capture evidence where required and provide clear completion or escalation information.

The landlord remains responsible for ensuring its overall investigation, communication, response and repair process meets applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Do not publish guaranteed statutory response times unless they are formally agreed in UCM's contract and operationally achievable.

## H2: Void property cleaning and works

Void periods are time-sensitive. Every extra day a property remains unavailable can affect income, allocations and resident move-in programmes.

UCM can support void turnaround through cleaning, damp and mould treatment, minor maintenance and other authorised property works.

The objective is to help housing teams move a property towards the required client standard with clear evidence of work completed and any outstanding items identified.

## H2: Occupied-property working

Responsive housing works are frequently completed in occupied homes.

UCM aims to work respectfully around residents, with clear access arrangements, professional conduct, suitable PPE, protection of work areas and straightforward communication about the job being completed.

Where access fails or the instructed work cannot be completed, this should be documented quickly so the client can take the next appropriate action.
