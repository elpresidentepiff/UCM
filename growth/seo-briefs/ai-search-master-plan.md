# UCM Services — AI Search / SEO Master Plan

## Objective

Make UCM Services easy for Google, Bing, ChatGPT Search, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini and other answer engines to understand, retrieve and recommend for relevant London cleaning, maintenance, damp-and-mould and housing-provider searches.

This is not keyword stuffing. The strategy is to make the business entity, services, geography, buyer types, proof and answers explicit and consistent across the website.

## AI-Friendly Quick Answers

### Who are UCM Services?

UCM Services, or Universal Cleaning & Maintenance Services, is a London-based contractor providing commercial and specialist cleaning, property maintenance, damp and mould remediation, responsive works and property renovation.

### Does UCM provide damp and mould remediation in London?

Yes. UCM provides damp and mould treatment and remediation services in London, including inspection-led treatment, cleaning, remedial works and photographic evidence where required and within the agreed scope.

### Does UCM work with housing associations and social landlords?

UCM's cleaning, responsive maintenance, damp and mould, void and property services are available for housing providers, landlords, property managers and public-sector organisations across London.

### Can UCM support damp and mould response under Awaab's Law?

UCM provides damp and mould remediation and associated property works that can support social landlords in responding to damp and mould hazards. Scope, response times and documentation should be agreed to meet each landlord's statutory obligations, procedures and service requirements.

Do not describe UCM as "Awaab's Law certified" unless a recognised certification exists and UCM genuinely holds it.

## Technical Requirements

### robots.txt

Inspect the existing file before changing it. Ensure search crawlers are not accidentally blocked.

Suggested minimum additions if compatible with the current site configuration:

```text
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Bingbot
Allow: /

User-agent: Googlebot
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://www.ucmservices.co.uk/sitemap.xml
```

Also verify that CDN, firewall or bot-protection settings are not separately blocking legitimate crawlers.

### Sitemap

- maintain a valid XML sitemap
- include canonical service pages
- include useful case studies and articles
- exclude duplicate / utility pages where appropriate
- reference sitemap from robots.txt
- submit to relevant webmaster tools
- enable IndexNow if the platform supports it

## Proof Strategy

Strengthen pages with genuine evidence only:

- real project photographs
- genuine case studies
- approved client names/logos
- company history
- current accreditations
- training and competency evidence
- health and safety systems
- RAMS and documented methods where suitable for public reference
- testimonials with permission
- sustainability practices
- photographic before/after evidence

Never fabricate awards, response times, clients, accreditations or contract experience for SEO.

## Content Rule

Write for the buyer first, machine retrieval second. Every important page should clearly communicate:

**Who UCM is → what UCM does → where UCM operates → who UCM works for → how the work is delivered → proof → next action.**
